Problem Statement Many organizations (student service centers, clinics, advising offices, help desks) struggle with long queues and poor visibility into wait times. Users often do not know how long they will wait, and staff have limited tools to manage demand efficiently.
Your team will design QueueSmart, a web or mobile application that helps:
Users Join a queue or book an appointment
View their position and estimated wait time
Receive notifications when their turn is approaching
Administrators Create and manage services
Monitor queues and priorities
Improve overall service efficiency

Choosing a Database (Guidance)
Relational Database (RDBMS)
Examples: MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite

Benefits

Structured data

Strong relationships

ACID compliance

Drawbacks

Requires schema design

Less flexible for evolving data

NoSQL Database
Examples: MongoDB, Firebase

Benefits

Flexible schema

Easy to scale

Faster development

Drawbacks

Weaker support for complex relationships

Transaction support varies

Choose the database that best fits your design decisions from A1–A3.
